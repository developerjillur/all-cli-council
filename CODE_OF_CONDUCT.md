# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Argue with the work, not the person.

## What this means here

**Disagreement is the product.** This repo exists because five models that agree tell you less
than five that split. The same is true of people — a review that finds nothing has usually not
looked. Say the uncomfortable thing.

**Bring evidence.** *"This is wrong"* is a start; *"this is wrong, here is the command that
shows it"* is a contribution. Claims here are expected to carry the measurement or say they
are a judgement call.

**Being wrong is not a failure.** Almost every control in this repo was wrong on its first
version, and the record says so on purpose. Someone correcting you has done you a favour.

**Do not perform.** No hype in issues, no invented numbers in benchmarks, no "this changes
everything". A tool that oversells itself is harder to trust about its limits.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind. Deliberately misrepresenting
someone's work or words. Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer via an issue or email. Reports are handled privately. Anything from a
correction to a permanent ban, proportionate to what happened.
